Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Cancellation

All services are month-to-month. You may cancel at any time by emailing support@ecom-david.com. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing month, and you will not be billed again.

2. Refunds

Because our services are labor performed on your accounts, fees for work already completed are not refundable. If you cancel before any work has started on a paid invoice, we will refund that invoice in full. If you believe you were billed in error, contact us within 30 days and we will review and correct it.

3. Advertising spend

Advertising spend is paid directly to Google or other platforms from your own account. We cannot refund ad spend charged by those platforms.

4. How refunds are issued

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 10 business days.
