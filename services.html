Services

All services are delivered online by ECOMDAVID LLC. Pricing is quoted in writing after a free review of your store.

Google Merchant Center Setup & Compliance

We set up new Merchant Center accounts and help existing accounts meet Google's Shopping policies. We review your website, store policies and product feed, make the needed changes, and request a review from Google.

  • New Merchant Center account setup
  • Shipping, return and privacy policy pages
  • Business and contact information checks
  • Product disapproval fixes (price, availability, GTIN, images)

Google Ads Management

We build and manage Search, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns for online stores.

  • Account setup or restructuring
  • Conversion tracking (GA4 and Google Ads)
  • Ongoing campaign and budget management
  • Weekly written reports

Product Feed Optimization

We clean up and improve your product feed so products are approved and shown to the right shoppers.

  • Feed audit and cleanup
  • Supplemental feeds
  • Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce and other platforms
