Services

Get approved. Then get profitable.

Our main focus is Google: getting your store approved on Google Merchant Center and running Google Ads that actually make money. Everything else supports that.

01 · Main Service

Google Merchant Center Approval & Suspension Fixes

If your Merchant Center account is suspended, disapproved or stuck, we find out why and fix it. We check your website, policies and product feed against Google's Shopping policies, make the needed changes, and submit a clear review request.

  • Misrepresentation and “website needs improvement” suspensions
  • Shipping, return, refund and privacy policy pages written and set up
  • Business information, contact details and checkout trust checks
  • Product disapprovals: price, availability, GTIN, images, landing pages
  • Correct review request — submitted only once issues are fixed
  • New Merchant Center setup for stores starting from scratch
02 · Main Service

Google Ads Management

Search, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns built around your real profit — after product costs, shipping and returns — not just the ROAS Google reports.

  • New account setup or full rebuild of wasted-spend accounts
  • Conversion tracking set up and verified (GA4, Google Ads, enhanced conversions)
  • Non-branded growth as the main goal — not just your own brand name
  • Daily monitoring and budget pacing by a senior specialist
  • Written weekly reports in plain English
03 · Supports Google

Shopping Feed & Performance Max

Most Shopping campaigns underperform because of the product feed, not the campaign. We fix titles, attributes and categories so Google shows the right product to the right search.

  • Feed audit, cleanup and supplemental feeds
  • Performance Max asset groups organized by profit margin
  • Search term control to protect your budget
  • Works with Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce and more
