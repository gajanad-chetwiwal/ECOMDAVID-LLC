FAQ

Common questions

Can you guarantee Merchant Center approval?

No honest agency can — Google makes the final decision. What we do is find every issue Google is likely flagging, fix it properly, and only then request a review. That gives your store the best possible chance.

How long does Merchant Center approval take?

Fixes usually take a few days to two weeks, depending on how much needs to change on your website and feed. Google's review itself typically takes several business days after submission.

Do I need a Merchant Center account to run Google Ads?

For Search ads, no. For Shopping and Performance Max ads — where your products appear with images and prices — yes, an approved Merchant Center account is required.

Which platforms do you work with?

Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Wix, Squarespace and most custom stores.

How much does it cost?

The audit is free. After that, pricing depends on the work needed and your ad spend. We'll give you a clear quote with the audit — no surprises.
